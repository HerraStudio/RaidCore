Adapted for Tactical Inventory: static GLB mesh/node transforms converted to OBJ,
scaled twice the initial 0.96-block fit, front oriented north. Original PNG preserved.
Minecraft provides standard lighting instead of the source GLB's PBR shading.

In Blender 5.2.2, reposed the door closed and authored a 60-degree hinge-opening
action for door, handle and lock, holding open. Exported closed mesh parts and
the actual Blender curve for runtime playback.
